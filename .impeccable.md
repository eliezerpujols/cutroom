## Design Context

### Users
Eli, a marketing content creator, editing an already-generated vertical reel locally.
The jobs are trimming, splitting, moving and slipping clips, extracting audio, adjusting
audio independently, and exporting the result. Originals must stay intact.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, calm, capable. Give the creator confidence without a professional editing
console's density. Explain slip in everyday language when that tool is selected.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the system theme by default, with charcoal dark and light options, as explicitly
requested. Pure white and neutral gray light surfaces, neutral charcoal dark surfaces, restrained color accents on controls and tracks, flat work surfaces, generous preview space,
legible thumbnail tracks and real audio waveforms. Locally bundled DM Sans and Manrope.
Mobbin references inspected: VEED (large preview, thin timeline) and Vimeo (filmstrip
clips and contextual controls). Adapt their hierarchy, not their full tool inventory.

### Design Principles
1. The video and timeline are the main workspace.
2. Show properties for the selected clip only; advanced controls expand on demand.
3. Edits are reversible, saved locally, and never change the source file.
4. Use text with unfamiliar actions, accessible tooltips, keyboard shortcuts and visible focus.
5. Keep the whole job available at narrow widths through panel tabs and timeline scrolling.
